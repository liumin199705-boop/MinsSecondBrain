# Q05 — agreed 19 October 2026

## Refined question

A pool administrator proposes new governance and cost rules for a pool that licenses BBC patents. How would you assess it and advise the BBC?

## Agreed answer

I would first compare the proposed rules with the existing agreement and establish how changes can validly be made. I would check the administrator’s authority, the BBC’s voting or consent rights, and any deadline for responding or objecting.

I would then assess the practical effects on the BBC. For governance, I would examine changes to voting power, representation and the administrator’s discretion, particularly over licensing and enforcement decisions. For costs, I would check how charges are calculated and allocated, whether new expenditure needs approval, and the effect on the BBC’s net licensing income.

I would also review reporting and audit rights, so the BBC can understand and verify the charges. I would check withdrawal rights and what obligations or licences would continue after exit.

With the senior patent lawyer and relevant business colleagues, I would assess whether the changes support the BBC’s objectives, including appropriate licensing income and adoption of its technology, while respecting any relevant standards commitments.

I would recommend acceptance or targeted amendments, with clear reasons. If the proposals were materially adverse, I would consider the available voting, objection or dispute routes and assess the consequences of withdrawal before recommending it.
